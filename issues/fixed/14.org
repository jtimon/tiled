** FIXED #14 Branching undo history and tree navigation

branches, atomic commands/callbacks, timed typing/deletion groups, shared-view
point adjustment, saved-content dirty tracking and a selection-only tree browser.

M-x exposes undo/redo and branch choice without capturing terminal child keys.

including in-memory history lifetime, fresh history on restore and no pruning.
